## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end corporate gifting for the Vietnamese Lunar New Year (Tết Đinh Mùi 2027). It fuses traditional celebratory heritage with contemporary luxury e-commerce. The aesthetic is curated for discerning B2B enterprises, executives, and high-net-worth clients seeking prestigious, bespoke gift hampers and experiential cultural offerings.

The interface pairs deep ceremonial lacquered tones with refined metallic radiance. The visual direction leverages atmospheric glassmorphism over rich, dark backgrounds, creating an immersive, nocturnal salon atmosphere punctuated by warm, glowing accents. The experience evokes exclusivity, prosperity, and meticulous craftsmanship without falling into commercial cliché or visual noise.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an evocative deep wine, burgundy, and imperial maroon spectrum, accented by multi-dimensional royal golds that mimic metallic foil stamping.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** The root backdrop is anchored by `#1A0507` (Deep Obsidian Maroon), layered with `#3D0A0B` and `#4A080C` for raised surfaces, card backgrounds, and navigation bars. Highlight boundaries utilize `#5C0B11` to simulate lacquered wooden casket edges.
- **Royal Gold Accents:** Secondary accents use vibrant, lustrous golds (`#F5C542` base, shading into `#D97706` amber and `#FBBF24`). Gradients transition linearly at 135 degrees from `#FDE68A` through `#F5C542` down to `#B45309`.
- **Text & Editorial Hierarchy:** High-contrast display copy is rendered in pure white `#FFFFFF` and pale champagne cream `#FEF3C7`. Secondary copy, metadata, and supporting labels rest in warm gold-tinted neutrals `#FDE68A` and subdued sandstone `#D1A77E`.
- **Borders & Overlays:** Translucent gold lines employ `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.25)` to `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.45)`, maintaining crisp physical boundaries without harsh solid transitions.

## Typography

The type system balances Vietnamese cultural grandeur with clean modern legibility. 

- **Playfair Display** commands the editorial narrative: hero headings, catalogue suite titles, and decorative year designations ("Đinh Mùi 2027"). Its high-contrast serifs, sweeping terminals, and graceful italic styles reference classical prestige and heritage typography.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers precise, legible UI execution. Selected for clean geometry and open apertures, it ensures Vietnamese diacritics (dấu ngã, hỏi, nặng) render clearly across dense B2B quotation tables, product specifications, and checkout flows.
- Small labels and eyebrow tags utilize upper-cased `label-sm` and `label-md` with expanded letter spacing (+0.08em to +0.12em) to evoke luxury packaging embossing.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop viewports, condensing to an 8-column layout on tablet (768px - 1024px) and a 4-column layout on mobile (<768px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1280px` to maintain focused visual density and preserve white space.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px base grid. Generous vertical section offsets (`5rem` to `7.5rem` on desktop) prevent visual crowding, giving gift boxes and curated collections center stage. Component interiors follow concise geometric steps: compact badges and pills rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while luxury hero card panels apply `space-lg` and `space-xl` internally to evoke bespoke packaging unboxing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism set against deep lacquered surfaces, reinforced by delicate gold ambient glows:

- **Base Canvas:** Solid, non-reflective `#1A0507`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Lists, Product Tiles):** Background composed of `rgba(61, 10, 11, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Bordered by a razor-thin 1px stroke of `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.25)`. Shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Modals, Dropdowns, Hovered Tiles):** Surface switches to `rgba(74, 8, 12, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border brightens to `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.45)`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 25px rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Bars & Overlays):** Semi-opaque dark core `rgba(26, 5, 7, 0.92)` accented with continuous dual-layer illumination: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` paired with a soft radial gold halo `0 0 20px rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, tailored corner treatment (`roundedness: 1`), reflecting the craftsmanship of beveled wooden gift caskets and rigid silk-wrapped boxes. Standard elements use `0.25rem` (4px), containers and cards scale to `0.5rem` (8px), while large modal dialogs cap at `0.75rem` (12px). Pill radii are strictly reserved for status badges, availability indicators, and interactive floating bar items.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Styled as radiant gold ingots. Background is a vibrant linear gradient from `#FDE68A` through `#F5C542` to `#D97706`. Text is dark burgundy `#240406` with font weight `600`. Hover activates an outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.5)`. Transitions occur over `200ms ease-out`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `rgba(61, 10, 11, 0.4)` with a 1px border of `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.5)`. Text in `#FEF3C7`. Hover transitions the background to `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.15)` and border color to `#F5C542`.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text with warm gold coloration `#FDE68A`, accompanied by a micro-arrow icon translating +4px horizontally on hover.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Select Fields:** Background tinted at `rgba(26, 5, 7, 0.7)` with an inset border of `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.2)`. Placeholder rendered in `#A88277`. 
- **Focus State:** Border sharpens to `#F5C542` with an ambient glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Dark square/circular base with 1px gold border. Checked state renders a solid gold fill `#F5C542` with an imperial maroon `#3D0A0B` check glyph.

### Cards & Catalogue Tiles
- Constructed using Level 1 Glassmorphic styling. Image frames feature an inner border inset to simulate silk picture framing.
- Price displays feature Playfair Display in `#FEF3C7`, accompanied by small, uppercase B2B VAT / order tiers in `#D1A77E`.

### Badges & Chips
- Status indicators (e.g., "Giới Hạn 2027", "Pre-order VIP") utilize a semi-transparent burgundy pill (`rgba(92, 11, 17, 0.8)`) outlined with a 1px `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.35)` rim and gold typography.

### Floating Quick Contact Bar
- Fixed centrally to the bottom viewport (`bottom: 1.5rem`).
- Formed from a pill-shaped glass dock with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, background `rgba(26, 5, 7, 0.85)`, and a complete border in `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.4)`.
- Houses direct B2B corporate hotline, Zalo VIP channel, and corporate brochure PDF download triggers, with dividers rendered in vertical strokes of `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.2)`.
